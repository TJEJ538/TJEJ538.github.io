About

Research shaped by classroom practice

I am an educational technology and language-learning researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer-assisted language learning, argumentative writing, and collaborative knowledge building.

Before entering higher education, I spent more than a decade teaching English in secondary schools. That experience continues to shape my research: technology should support better thinking, stronger dialogue, and more meaningful learning rather than simply automate the work learners need to do themselves.

My current work examines question-only AI Socratic dialogue, AI-supported writing environments, human–AI co-creative learning, and learning analytics.
